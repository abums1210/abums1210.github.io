### Features of GTA III Rect-HUD (v1.2):
* Rectangular Radar and HUD styling based on GTA V.
* Simple Weapon and Radio wheel.
* Stylish timer, location and vehicle text at bottom right similar to GTA V.
* Font style of zone/vehicle customizable.
* GTA V style weapon/radar icons and fonts.
* Smaller message box at top left similar to GTA V.
* Mobile phone which shows time.
* Config file to adjust the various HUD elements.
* Config file to adjust colors of HUD to various styles.
